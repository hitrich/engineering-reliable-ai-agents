A bounded agent loop

The model proposes; the runtime checks arguments and authority; the tool supplies data; the runtime saves observations, checks budgets and either continues or stops. Terminal states include answered, needs approval, stopped and failed. A text answer is not a verified business effect.

Approval binds one exact operation

The action service stores an exact proposal and evidence snapshot. A reviewer approves its digest. Commit rechecks current scope, original reviewer, expiry, integrity, evidence and ticket version inside the local transaction. A repeated key returns the protected original receipt; it does not repeat the effect.

Success at least once differs from success every time

This calculator assumes a fixed probability p and independent trials. It illustrates exact arithmetic. It is not an estimate of Northstar performance, and related benchmark trials often violate the independence assumption.

All k trials succeed: pk
At least one succeeds: 1 − (1 − p)k
0%50%100%

Use one currency consistently. These are hypothetical inputs, not model prices or observed business results. Include failed work and retries in machine cost; include review and correction time in human work. Verified resolutions are a separate count from correctly handled escalations.
